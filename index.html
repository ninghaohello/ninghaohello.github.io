Background

I am an assistant professor in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Georgia. I received my Ph.D. degree in Computer Science from Texas A&M University in 2021, under the supervision of Dr. Xia Hu. I received my M.S. degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Georgia Institute of Technology in 2015 and B.S. degrees in Information Engineering from South China University of Technology in 2014.
