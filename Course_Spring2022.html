Course Description

The goal of this course is deriving a comprehensive understanding of fundamental issues, techniques, applications and future directions of data mining. This course presents a rigorous overview of methods for machine learning, dimension reduction, modeling methods for images, texts and graphs, and industry applications including outlier detection and recommender systems.
